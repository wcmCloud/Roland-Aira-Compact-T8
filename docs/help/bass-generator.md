# Bass generator

Writes a new bass line into the current pattern, up to its last step, following a key and a scale.

- **KEY:** C to B.
- **SCALE:** major, the minors, the pentatonics, blues and the modes (Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Locrian, Phrygian dominant).
- **CHORDS:** a progression built from the scale (for example i – VI – III – VII), and how many steps each chord lasts.

## Knobs

- **From octave** and **Octaves:** the range of the line (octave 1 is the classic bass range).
- **Notes:** how often a step plays a new note (beats get a bonus).
- **Chord tones:** how often a note on the beat is the chord's root, third or fifth.
- **Ties, Slides, Accents, Octave jumps:** how often each happens.

The line starts on the root and each chord change starts on that chord's root. **Generate**, **Randomize**, **Undo** and **Seed** work as in the rhythm generator.
