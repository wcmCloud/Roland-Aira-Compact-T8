# Loading and sending patterns

The T-8 doesn't send its patterns over MIDI. Instead it shows up on the computer as a USB drive named **T-8**, with a `BACKUP` folder (its current 64 patterns) and a `RESTORE` folder (patterns to load).

## Drive mode

- Switch the T-8 **off** and connect it by USB.
- **Hold PLAY and switch it on**; keep holding until the step buttons light up.
- Wait about a minute, until the step buttons stop counting and the **T-8** drive appears.

**Load from T-8** and **Write to T-8** are only enabled while the drive is connected; the dot next to them turns green. The editor looks for the drive when it starts and when you come back to its window; **Check** looks again. In drive mode the T-8 isn't a MIDI device: switch it off and on normally to play it.

## Loading

- Press **Load from T-8**: the 64 patterns from `BACKUP` appear in the pattern grid.
- Press **Save as…** to keep a copy on the computer, then eject the drive and switch the T-8 off.

## Sending back

- **Save** your patterns on the computer first.
- With the T-8 in drive mode, press **Write to T-8** and confirm: all 64 patterns go into `RESTORE`.
- **Eject** the T-8 drive, then press **ACCENT** on the T-8.
- Wait until the display shows `dOnE` before switching it off.

Writing replaces **all 64 patterns** on the T-8, so load and save its current patterns first if you want to keep them.

## Factory reset

Hold **SLIDE (WRITE)** while switching on, press **ACCENT**, wait for `dOnE`. This erases every pattern on the T-8 and puts the factory patterns back.
