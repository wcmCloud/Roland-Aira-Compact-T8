# Rhythm editing

Each row is one of the T-8's tracks (Accent, Bass drum, Snare, Tom, Clap, Closed hat, Open hat) and each column one of 32 steps; the brighter columns are the beats.

## Steps

- Click a cell to turn it on or off. The cell you clicked is outlined in white and shown in the orange **step panel** on the right.
- **ON** switches the step; **Velocity** goes from v.0 to v.10 (the T-8 uses v.7 for new notes); **Probability** is how often the step plays, as on the T-8.
- **Sub step:** Half, Third or Quarter play 2, 3 or 4 hits in the step; Flam adds a quiet hit just before. Sub steps have their own probability.

## Pattern knobs

- **Last step** (1–32): steps after it are dimmed and don't play.
- **Shuffle** (0–200): delays every second 16th note.
- **Flam** (0–100): the spacing of a flam's two hits.

## Rows

- Click a row's name to select it (outlined orange).
- The icons at the end of a row **copy** it, **paste** a copied row over it (also into another pattern or bank) and **clear** it.
- With a row selected, **Cmd/Ctrl+C** and **Cmd/Ctrl+V** copy and paste.

## Several steps at once

**Ctrl-click** (Cmd-click on macOS) or **Shift-click** active steps to pick them; they get a blue outline. **Select all active steps** picks every active step. The blue panel then sets Velocity, Probability and Sub step for all of them: **Apply** writes them and clears the selection, **Clear** leaves the steps as they were.
