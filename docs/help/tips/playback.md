# Playback
<!-- topic: getting-started -->
Play, Stop and Pause at the BPM on the knob. **Output** is Auto, T-8, or Offline. Auto plays on this computer when the T-8 isn't available. **Follow clock** waits for an external MIDI clock. **Fill** plays the fill once after this loop; **Select on T-8** switches the T-8 to this pattern.

**Audition** plays steps as you edit them; **Show playing step** outlines the playing step in yellow. What Play does is set in the MIDI settings.
