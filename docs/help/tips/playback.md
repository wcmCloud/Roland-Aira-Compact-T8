# Playback
<!-- topic: getting-started -->
Play, Stop and Pause. **Output** is Auto, T-8, or Offline. Offline adds a kit, volume and **Sound settings**. Auto plays here when the T-8 isn't available. **Follow clock** waits for an external MIDI clock. **Fill** plays the fill once; **Select on T-8** switches the T-8 to this pattern.

**Audition** plays steps as you edit them; **Show playing step** outlines the playing step in yellow. Play on the T-8 is set in the MIDI settings.
