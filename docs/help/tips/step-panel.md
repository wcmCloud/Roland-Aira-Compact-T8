# Step panel
<!-- topic: rhythm-editing -->
Edits the step you clicked (orange) or, after Ctrl/Cmd/Shift-clicking several, all of them (blue, then **Apply**).

**Velocity** v.0–v.10 (the T-8 uses v.7), **Probability** how often it plays, **Sub step** 2, 3 or 4 hits in the step or a flam, with its own probability.
