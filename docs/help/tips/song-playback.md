# Play area and loop
<!-- topic: song -->
**Play song** plays from the start; **Previous** and **Next** jump between entries. Click a block on the timeline to play that part next, as soon as the current pattern ends. The song uses the Playback card's **Output**, so Offline plays it on this computer.

**LOOP:** play once, loop the whole song, or keep repeating one entry. **Follow DAW MIDI clock** runs the song from a DAW's clock.
