# Bass generator
<!-- topic: bass-generator -->
Pick a **KEY**, a **SCALE** and **CHORDS**, set the knobs and press **Generate** to replace the line. It starts on the root.

**Vary** keeps the pitches and the rhythm. **LOCK ROOTS** keeps a root's octave on the beat; other notes can jump an octave. **Fill bank** writes eight related lines into the next empty slots.
