# Bass generator
<!-- topic: bass-generator -->
**KEY**, **SCALE** and **CHORDS** are on the step editor, above the keyboard. Opening a pattern sets the key and scale from the notes in the line. **Generate** writes a new line in that key. **COLOURS** starts off; turn it on to paint that scale on the keyboard.

**Vary** keeps the pitches and the rhythm. **LOCK ROOTS** keeps a root's octave on the beat; other notes can jump an octave. **Fill bank** writes eight related lines into the next empty slots.
