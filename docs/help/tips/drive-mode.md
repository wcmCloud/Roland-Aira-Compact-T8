# T-8 drive mode
<!-- topic: drive-mode -->
Load from T-8 and Write to T-8 need the T-8 as a USB drive: switch it off, hold PLAY while switching it on, wait about a minute for the **T-8** drive, then press **Check**.

After **Write to T-8**: eject the drive, press **ACCENT** on the T-8 and wait for dOnE.
