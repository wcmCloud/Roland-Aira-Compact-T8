# Step editor
<!-- topic: bass-editing -->
Click a step's note or number to select it, then click a key on the keyboard to give it that note. **COLOURS** starts off. Turn it on to paint the scale in bright keys and that step's chord in pink. Opening a pattern sets **KEY** and **SCALE** from its notes. Generate and Vary use that same key. With **Next step after a note** on, the selection moves on. The chevrons move the selection.
