# Rhythm generator
<!-- topic: rhythm-generator -->
Pick a **STYLE** and the lit instrument chips, set the knobs and press **Generate** to replace the pattern. The same settings and seed give the same pattern; **Randomize** tries a new seed.

**Vary** keeps locked parts (the kick by default) and rewrites hats, ghost notes and probability. **Fill bank** puts eight related patterns in the next empty slots.

**Live variation** gives hits a step probability, so the T-8 varies the pattern every loop.
