# Keyboard shortcuts

- **F1:** the help, at the topic for the tab you're on (the MIDI settings topic in the settings window).
- **Cmd/Ctrl+Z:** undo the last change to the open patterns. **Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z** redoes it. This includes a cell, a cleared or pasted row, a paste, a transpose, and Generate, Vary, Fill bank and Make fill.
- **Cmd/Ctrl+S:** save the patterns.
- **Cmd/Ctrl+C / Cmd/Ctrl+V** with a rhythm row selected: copy and paste the row.
- **Ctrl-click** (Cmd-click on macOS) or **Shift-click** rhythm steps: pick several to edit together.
- **Knobs:** arrow keys or the scroll wheel; drag with Shift for fine steps; double-click to reset.
- **Right-click** a bass step: every note, the state, Accent and Slide.
- **Esc** closes the MIDI settings and Help windows.
